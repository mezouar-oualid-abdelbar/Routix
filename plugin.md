## Plugins

**Status: in progress.**

Plugins will let you extend routix with custom routines and activity suggestions — for example, an AI coach plugin that recommends an activity for you to do based on your habits.

### Making your own plugin

Fork the [Routix-plugin template](https://github.com/mezouar-oualid-abdelbar/Routix-plugin), edit it to do what you want, then install it in the app by pasting your fork's GitHub URL into the Plugins screen.
